# Code of Conduct and Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** Jermaine F. Barker · **Effective:** 2026-08-26 · **Review:** annual

## Integrity and ethical values
Everyone working on JMCB platforms — owner, contractors, interns, partners with repo access — commits to: honesty in reporting status and incidents; respect for the people whose data we hold (patients, taxpayers, citizens, founders); no conflicts of interest with clients (Runwei, Polintel/CBCI; Guidehouse day-job boundaries respected); and compliance with the law in every jurisdiction we serve (US federal and states, Guyana, Trinidad & Tobago).

## Acceptable use of systems and data
1. Use JMCB systems and client data **only** for the engagement you are assigned to.
2. Never copy Confidential or Restricted data (Data Classification Policy) to personal devices, personal cloud storage, or unapproved AI tools. Approved AI tooling is listed in the Subprocessor Register.
3. Never commit secrets, real customer data, or real PHI/PII to any repository. Test data is synthetic.
4. Use your own named account; never share credentials; MFA is mandatory.
5. Report any suspected incident, lost device, or phishing within 1 hour to jermaine@jmcbtech.com.
6. Do not disable security controls (branch protection, scanners, logging) without written owner approval.
7. CaughtUp / IRC §7216: taxpayer information is used for tax-preparation assistance only — no marketing, analytics, or model training.

## Prohibited
Unauthorized access attempts, data exfiltration, installing unapproved software on systems holding Restricted data, using client data for personal benefit, harassment or discrimination.

## Acknowledgment
Acknowledged on onboarding (Onboarding policy §2) and annually. Violations lead to immediate access removal and may end the engagement.
